# Integrated process proof

Use this workflow when a periodic process must prove selection, generated Business Instances, human responses, recovery, schedules, watched changes and Test cleanup together. Read current Discovery for supported operations, result types, availability, authority and scope. Reuse its ordinary examples when suitable; keep business eligibility, scoring and recipient rules in Metadata and anonymized fixtures.

1. Define the business period, eligibility and processed-work exclusion. Keep protection against equivalent active work distinct from excluding already processed work on a later run. Prove a completed run followed by a newly eligible Business Instance and identical inputs, then establish the intended rules for the next period.
2. Observe generation, its stakeholder summary, outstanding responses and final completion separately. For partial generation, summarize committed effects and actual delivery outcomes from authorized durable detail. Completed iterations can undercount effects already committed by a failed iteration; expected request counts cannot establish delivery success. Never announce complete generation while recovery is still needed.
3. Recover within the admitted execution using currently available controls after correcting the cause. Inject a failure after a Business Instance or notification has committed, then verify retry adds only missing work. Preserve early responses, exact request membership and the original denominator across restart and reassignment. An unrelated Business Instance sharing the period must not change completion. Exercise the chosen deadline outcome without treating non-response as an answer or approval.
4. Contrast independent selected-instance Actions with iteration inside one execution. Retain each accepted reference and verify its outcome separately. Test assigned-participant summaries and delegated oversight while confirming that linked Business Data and administrative controls retain their own authority.
5. Exercise the configured recurring summary and review missed occurrences independently. Verify current recovery/skip controls and a newer occurrence while an older one remains unresolved. For watched changes, prove a qualifying committed transition with original causal provenance, then unrelated attributes, repeated values and a qualifying rolled-back transition that starts nothing. Business response membership stays independent of informational summary counts.
6. Run the equivalent definition explicitly against actual Test fixtures with eligible Test Users and the supported logical initiator choice. Verify fixed Test scope, unchanged Real data and retained actual requester attribution. Complete one run, then cancel and purge another only through current administrative availability and the agreed cleanup intent. Verify fixtures and committed effects survive, and late responses or delivery cannot revive purged work.

Completion: one coherent API journey establishes these outcomes through current Discovery; business rules remain Metadata, recovery preserves committed work, and independent existing failures or unavailable support are stated rather than hidden by example-specific behavior.
